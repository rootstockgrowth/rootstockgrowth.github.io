What Is a New Lawn Care Customer Worth?

A new lawn care customer is worth $1,368 in the first year by Rootstock Growth's 2026 figures: 24 weekly mowings at $57. That uses the shortest regional season, about 24 weeks in the Northeast; in the Southeast, 45 mowings make the year $2,565. A fertilizer application is $236.

How much is a new lawn care customer worth?

A new lawn care customer brings in $1,368 over one season of weekly mowing, by Rootstock Growth's count, before any fertilizer. The mowing route carries the year: it comes to more than five times a single $236 fertilizer job.

Why is a lawn care customer worth more than the first cut?

A lawn customer is worth $1,368 for the season against $57 for the first cut, as Rootstock Growth counts it, because the lawn is mowed weekly for 24 weeks. Those 24 weeks are the shortest regional season, the Northeast's. The first visit is only one twenty-fourth of what that customer pays over the year.

What is a lawn care customer worth in the Southeast?

In the Southeast a lawn customer brings in $2,565 a year in Rootstock Growth's figures, since the season there runs 45 mowings at the same $57. That is nearly twice the Northeast's 24-mowing year.

What is one fertilizer application worth to a lawn care company?

A single fertilizer application is worth $236 in Rootstock Growth's 2026 figures, more than four mowing visits at $57.

How do the mowing numbers multiply out?

A Northeast customer on weekly mowing comes to $1,368 by Rootstock Growth's figures: $57 × 24 mowings = $1,368.

The same customer on a Southeast schedule: $57 × 45 mowings = $2,565.

Same price per cut, two different years, and the only thing that changed was the count of mowings.
