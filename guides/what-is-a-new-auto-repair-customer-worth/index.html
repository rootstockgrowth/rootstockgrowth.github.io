What Is a New Auto Repair Customer Worth?

A new auto repair customer is worth $554 in the first year when the visit is a check-engine-light repair, according to Rootstock Growth's 2026 figures. Brake pads come in at $365 and an oil change at $174, so a first visit is worth $174 to $554.

How much does a household spend on car repair each year?

A household's yearly spending on car maintenance and repair comes to $984, according to Rootstock Growth. No single job in the table reaches that amount; the biggest, the $554 check-engine-light repair, covers a little over half of it.

Is a brake job worth much to a repair shop?

A brake pad replacement is worth $365 to an auto repair shop, by Rootstock Growth's count. That is about two-thirds of a check-engine-light repair at $554 and more than twice an oil change at $174.

What is one oil change worth to the shop?

An oil change is worth $174 to the shop in Rootstock Growth's figures, the smallest of the three jobs. It is under a fifth of the $984 a household spends on car maintenance and repair in a year.

How does an auto repair customer compare with other businesses?
