How much is a new solar customer worth?

A new solar customer is worth $30,712 to an installer for the first year, the median quoted price for a 12 kW rooftop system before incentives, according to Rootstock Growth. That is the quote before incentives, not what the homeowner pays after them.

What is one solar battery job worth to a solar installer?

A solar battery is worth about $15,000 to an installer in Rootstock Growth's 2026 figures, close to half the price of the panel system.
